Moye · Privacy Policy

A local-first Markdown reader for AI-written documents.

Effective: 2026-09-09

Short version: Moye has no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no data collection. Documents you open are handled entirely on your device and are never uploaded.

1. What data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data, documents, or usage information. There is no analytics or third-party tracking SDK bundled in the app.

2. How your documents are handled

Moye is a local-first Markdown reader. When you open a .md file (via the system file picker), it is read and rendered on your device only. Files stay where they are; they are never uploaded anywhere.

3. Permissions

  • The app only reads files you explicitly pick through the system document picker.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or network.

4. Third parties

We do not share data with any third party because we collect nothing. If a document references external resources (e.g. images), they are fetched only at your explicit request, and that access is governed by the respective services' own policies.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open a new issue at https://github.com/ninja9998877/moye-privacy/issues — it's the most reliable way to reach us.

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the effective date above reflects the current version.
