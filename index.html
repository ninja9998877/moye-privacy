Moye · Privacy Policy

墨页（"Moye"）— 本地 Markdown 阅读器 · Local Markdown reader

Effective: 2026-09-09 · 生效日期: 2026-09-09

Short version / 一句话总结: Moye has no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no data collection. Documents you open are handled entirely on your device. Moye 没有账号、没有追踪、没有分析、不收集任何数据。你打开的文件 完全在你自己的设备上处理。

1. What data we collect · 我们收集什么

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data, documents, or usage information. There is no analytics or third-party tracking SDK in the app.

我们不收集、不存储、不传输、也无权访问你的任何个人数据、文档或使用信息。应用内不含任何分析或第三方追踪 SDK。

2. How your documents are handled · 文档如何处理

Moye is a local-first Markdown reader. When you open a .md file via the system file picker, it is read and rendered on your device only. Files stay where they are; they are never uploaded anywhere.

Moye 是 本地优先 的 Markdown 阅读器。当你通过系统文件选择器打开 .md 文件时，它只在你的设备上被读取和渲染。文件留在原位，绝不上传到任何地方。

3. Permissions · 权限

  • The app only reads files you explicitly pick through the system document picker.
  • 应用只读取你通过系统文档选择器主动选择的文件。
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or network.
  • 不访问通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风或网络。

4. Third parties · 第三方

We do not share data with any third party, because we collect nothing. If the app links to external resources (e.g. images in a Markdown file), they are fetched only at your explicit request and your access is subject to those services' own policies.

我们不与任何第三方共享数据，因为我们不收集任何数据。若文档中的外部资源（如图片）在你明确请求时被加载，其访问受相应服务自身政策约束。

5. Contact · 联系我们

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this repository, or contact ninja9998877@users.noreply.github.com.

对本政策有疑问：在本仓库提 issue，或联系 ninja9998877@users.noreply.github.com。

We may update this policy as the app changes; the effective date above reflects the current version. 随应用更新本政策可能调整，以上生效日期对应当前版本。
